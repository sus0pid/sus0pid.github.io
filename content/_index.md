I build secure, fast networked systems. My work sits where cryptography meets systems and networking: post-quantum TLS, encryption for datacenter transports, and anonymous communication.

I am currently a research engineer intern at [Cloudflare](https://research.cloudflare.com), working on post-quantum data residency solutions. I received my PhD from the University of Edinburgh in 2026, advised by [Michio Honda](https://micchie.net). Before that I worked on anonymity networks with [Tariq Elahi](https://sites.google.com/view/tariqelahi/home), studied at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics with [Zhe Liu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Em0jNiUAAAAJ&hl=en), and visited [Pawel Szalachowski](https://scholar.google.pl/citations?user=kbU4kW8AAAAJ&hl=en) at SUTD.

Outside work I like cycling, trying new recipes, and reading psychology books.
